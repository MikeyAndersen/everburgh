# Concept art prompts — Temporal Isolation Labs

Til Midjourney / DALL·E / Firefly / Stable Diffusion. Prompterne er på **engelsk**, fordi
billedmodeller er trænet på engelsk — det er værktøjsinput, ikke fortælling.

Fælles stilanker, sæt den ind i alle fire hvis du vil have et sammenhængende sæt:

> *dark fantasy concept art, painterly, cinematic lighting, muted teal and violet palette
> against warm rust accents, high detail, moody atmosphere, no text, no watermark*

---

## 1 — Establishing shot: hele kammeret

Det billede du viser først. Skal sælge **skala** og **hjerteslaget**.

```
A vast vaulted underground laboratory chamber, ceiling lost in darkness. Hundreds of tall
cylindrical glass tanks line every wall in endless rows, filled with luminous green fluid,
each containing a single unconscious human figure suspended mid-float. Thin threads of pale
light stream inward from every tank toward the centre of the room. At the centre, a massive
black crystal the size of a carriage hovers ten feet above a polished metal floor, absorbing
light rather than casting it, faint violet fracture lines across its surface. A lone robed
figure stands before it, tiny against its mass. Ozone haze, volumetric god-rays bending
toward the crystal. Dark fantasy concept art, painterly, cinematic, wide establishing shot,
muted teal and violet palette, high detail, no text.
```

## 2 — De sovende: tankene tæt på

Til det øjeblik en spiller går hen og kigger. **Ikke fredeligt.**

```
Close view of three tall glass containment tanks filled with glowing green preservation
fluid. Inside each, a human prisoner floats unconscious in ragged clothing, eyes moving
rapidly beneath closed lids, fingers curled tight, one mouth open in a silent scream.
Copper cabling and brass fittings crown each tank. Faint threads of pale light draw out of
their temples and drift away into the dark. Condensation on the glass, sickly underlighting
from below. Dark fantasy concept art, painterly, claustrophobic, horror undertone, muted
teal palette, high detail, no text.
```

## 3 — Malcer ved krystallen

**Ingen kappesving, ingen magisk lyn.** Han skal ligne en ingeniør, ikke en troldmand.

```
A calm middle-aged archmage in a grey robe with deep red trim, standing composed with hands
clasped behind his back, facing a colossal hovering black crystal that drinks the light
around it. His expression is cold, analytical, faintly tired — a man interrupted during
important work, not a villain mid-monologue. Two hovering humanoid constructs of brass and
dark iron flank him at the edge of frame, faceless, gears turning at their joints, feet not
touching the ground. Violet backlight rims his silhouette. Dark fantasy concept art,
painterly, cinematic, restrained, muted violet and rust palette, high detail, no text.
```

## 4 — Charge 0: hjertet standser

Gem den til slutningen. Vis den **efter** Robs sætning, ikke før.

```
A colossal black crystal splitting apart from within, brilliant white-violet light forcing
through widening fractures. Dozens of human faces surface momentarily in the crystal's black
surface and sink away again like drowning swimmers, mouths open. Below it a metal laboratory
floor is buckling, glass tanks along the walls bursting in sequence, green fluid and shards
flooding outward. Backlit debris hanging in the air. Dark fantasy concept art, painterly,
cinematic, catastrophic, violent light, high contrast, high detail, no text.
```

## Bonus — næste session, hvis Bastillen falder

```
A colossal hexagonal floating citadel of brass and grey stone, half of its mass rendered in
impossible rigid geometry, breaking apart along a jagged planar rift as it falls from the
sky toward a dense city district below. Orange-tiled rooftops and open portal arches in the
streets beneath. Debris and light spilling from the fracture. Dark fantasy concept art,
epic scale, painterly, cinematic, dramatic sky, high detail, no text.
```
