# Houserules

These override 2014 RAW. Anything not listed here is RAW — and the "Deliberately RAW"
section below means we actually checked, not that we forgot.

## Combat

**Critical hits.** The player chooses, per crit: double the damage dice, or double the total.

> [!question] Open
> Doubling the total is strictly better than doubling the dice whenever the attack has a
> damage modifier — `2d8+10` vs `2d8+5`. The choice therefore collapses to "always double
> the total". If the intent was a real tradeoff, the second option probably wants to be
> *max the first die and roll the rest*. Confirm with the DM before relying on this.

**Initiative.** Rolled once at the start of combat and fixed for the whole fight. Never rerolled.

**Flanking.** Not used. No advantage, no bonus. Positioning matters for cover and movement only.

**Death saves.** Players track their own HP and death saves. High trust — the DM does not
track PC hit points at all. Do not ask a player to prove their HP total.

## Combat knowledge checks

A player may ask the DM **any** question in combat — how something works, what would happen
if X, what a creature is doing. This is always allowed and always costs nothing but a check.

The **ability they choose** sets the angle of the answer. The **roll** sets the depth:

| Roll | Result |
|------|--------|
| under 10 | Something true, but pointing the wrong way. **Never dead air.** |
| 10–14 | Confirmation that something is there. No mechanism. |
| 15–19 | The mechanism named. They understand *what* it is, not *what to do*. |
| 20+ | The lever, stated plainly, as the character's own realisation. |

> [!important] The sub-10 rule is load-bearing
> A failed check must still return something true. If failure gives nothing, players stop
> asking, and the whole rule dies. Misdirect — never stonewall.

Design consequence: encounters should be built so that **asking is the winning move**.
Put a sensory itch on each PC that is specific, strange, and useless on its own.

## Items and resources

**Healing potions.** Damage is rolled, never flat max.

- Stowed in a pack or elsewhere: **action** to drink.
- Carried on the belt: **bonus action** to drink.

PC notes should record where potions are carried, since it changes the action economy.

**Inspiration.** Not used at this table. Do not offer it, do not award it, do not build
encounters that assume it.

## Spellcasting

**Multiclass slots.** RAW. [[Sharn]] is Sorcerer/Warlock: Pact Magic slots stay entirely
separate from the Spellcasting slot table. Two pools, tracked independently.

**Sharn — Unstable Thread.** After using `Breach and Burn`, for 1 minute he rolls on the
Wild Magic Surge table on every **levelled spell** he casts. **Cantrips do not trigger it.**

He also has **Wild Magic Control**: rolls twice on the surge table and picks the result.

## Glyph powers

Frequency is **per glyph, as written** in each PC note. Not standardised.

| Glyph | Power | Frequency |
|-------|-------|-----------|
| Unwept Oath ([[Muk]]) | Rally of the Damned | as written |
| Breaching Vein ([[Sharn]]) | Breach and Burn | once per long rest |
| Broken Witness ([[Merith Lonehunter]]) | Rejection Pulse | **removed — no longer active** |

## Deliberately RAW

Checked and left alone: resting (8h long / 1h short), temporary HP stacking (does not stack),
encumbrance (not tracked in practice), monster critical hits.
