Tre eller flere høstede drømme der smelter sammen i fri luft, når en bank af Neuromantic
Decoy-tanke brister. Den har ikke ét ansigt. Den har alle ansigterne, og de taler ikke i takt.

### Traits

**Worn Face.** When a creature starts its turn within 30 feet, the Congeries takes on the
shape of something that creature fears. Only that creature sees it that way. The DM should
ask the player what their character is afraid of — and use it.

**Aura of Dread.** A creature that starts its turn within 20 feet makes a DC 15 Wisdom save
or is `Frightened` until the start of its next turn. On a success it is immune to this
Congeries' Aura of Dread for 24 hours.

**Amalgam.** When it takes 25 or more damage from a single source, one dream tears free:
a `Hollowed Dreamer` appears in an unoccupied space within 10 feet. This can happen at most
three times.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** Makes two `Rend the Sleeper` attacks and uses `Drink` if available.

**Rend the Sleeper.** Melee weapon attack: +8 to hit, reach 10 ft, one target.
*Hit:* 16 (3d6+6) slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) psychic damage.

**Drink (Recharge 5–6).** One creature it can see within 30 feet makes a DC 15 Charisma save.
On a failure the target takes 22 (4d10) psychic damage and the Congeries regains that many
hit points. On a success, half damage and no healing. A creature reduced to 0 hit points by
`Drink` is stable but cannot be woken for 1 hour by anything short of `Greater Restoration`.

### Reactions

**Recoil.** When it takes radiant damage, its Aura of Dread is suppressed until the end of
its next turn. Tell the party this the first time it happens — [[Muk]] should get to notice.
