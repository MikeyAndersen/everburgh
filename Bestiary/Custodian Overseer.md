## Stat block

**Armor Class:** 18 (Natural Armor)
**Hit Points:** 92 (12d8+36)
**Speed:** 30 ft, Fly 30 ft (hover)
**Damage Immunities:** Poison, Psychic
**Skills:** Perception +7

### Actions

**Multiattack.** Two `Logic Spike` attacks or two claw attacks.
**Logic Spike.** Ranged spell attack +8, 60 ft, 18 (4d8) psychic damage. Target makes a DC 16
Intelligence save or cannot use Reactions until the start of its next turn.
**Overseer's Command (3/day).** DC 16 Wisdom save. On a failure the target must use its Reaction
to immediately make one melee attack against an ally.

## Kampagne-noter

To af disse blev navngivet **Ananas** og **Cirkeline** ved bordet under kampen i den orange
bydel. De er også Malcers livvagter i [[Temporal Isolation Labs]].
