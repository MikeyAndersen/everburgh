## Stat block

**Armor Class:** 16 (Temporal Distortion)
**Hit Points:** 119 (14d8+56)
**Speed:** 30 ft, fly 40 ft (hover)

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 12 (+1) | 18 (+4) | 18 (+4) | 16 (+3) | 14 (+2) | 10 (+0) |

**Languages:** Forstår alle sprog, men taler kun i baglæns hvisken og lyden af knust glas.
**Challenge:** 8 (3,900 XP)

### Traits

**Amorphous.** Can move through openings as narrow as 1 inch without squeezing.
**Aura of Stagnant Time.** Enemies within 15 ft cannot use Reactions — no `Shield`, no
`Counterspell`, and notably no `Feather Fall`. The area is difficult terrain for everyone
except the Stalker.
**Shattered Visage.** Any enemy that starts its turn within 30 ft and can see its face makes a
DC 15 Wisdom save. On a failure they glimpse an unlived future or a predecessor's memory
(Rob, Hakim etc.) and have disadvantage on all attack rolls against the Stalker until the
start of their next turn.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** Two `Chronal Rend` attacks.
**Chronal Rend.** Melee spell attack, +7 to hit, reach 10 ft. Hit: 13 (2d8+4) slashing plus
9 (2d8) force. The target makes a DC 15 Constitution save or has disadvantage on Strength and
Dexterity checks until the end of its next turn.
**Erasure Gaze (Recharge 5–6).** 30-ft line, 5 ft wide. DC 15 Dexterity save.
*Fail:* 36 (8d8) force damage, and the target flickers out of the timeline — `Incapacitated`
and invisible until the start of the Stalker's next turn, then thrown back into the same square.
*Success:* half damage, no flicker.

### Reactions

**Temporal Sidestep.** When hit by an attack it can see, it pulls time back a few seconds and
halves all damage from that attack.
