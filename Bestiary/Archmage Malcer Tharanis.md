### Traits

**Planar Anchor.** Dissolves into light and resurrects 1 round later at half HP unless the
Planar Heart is destroyed.

### Actions

**Temporal Mastery (Recharge 5–6).** Freezes up to 2 creatures in time (DC 17 Wisdom save).

### Reactions

**Arcane Shielding.** Gains +3 AC as a reaction; deals 1d8 force damage on a hit.

### Legendary Actions (3/turn)

**Arcane Pulse.** Burst of force damage.
**Shift Reality.** Teleport and shift terrain.
**Temporal Step.** Teleport, leaving an explosive echo.

### Lair

**Lair Resonance.** Controls the machine room — Magnetic Pull, Gravity Shift, or Mana Drain.
