### Traits

**Sync-Link.** Advantage on attacks if at least one other Gearshaft Hound is within 5 ft of the
target and is not incapacitated.

### Actions

**Bite.** +7 to hit, 13 (2d8+4) piercing damage.
**Temporal Pounce.** If the hound moves 20 ft and hits with Bite, the target makes a DC 15
Strength save or is pushed 10 ft and `Stunned` until the end of its next turn.
