## Stat block

**Armor Class:** 20 (Plate, Shield)
**Hit Points:** 155 (18d10+54)
**Speed:** 30 ft
**Saving Throws:** Str +9, Con +7, Wis +6
**Damage Resistances:** Piercing, slashing and bludgeoning from nonmagical attacks

### Traits

**Aura of Absolute Order.** Hostile creatures within 20 ft cannot have advantage on attacks or
checks — the chaos in their movement is suppressed by his presence.

### Actions

**Multiattack.** Three attacks with Lawbringer's Hammer.
**Lawbringer's Hammer.** Melee weapon attack, +9 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 14 (2d8+5) bludgeoning
plus 9 (2d8) psychic. Target makes a DC 16 Strength save or is `Restrained` until the start of
the Justicare's next turn.
**Planar Anchor (Recharge 5–6).** Slams the hammer into the ground. All enemies within 30 ft make
a DC 16 Charisma save or are locked to their current plane — no teleportation, no portal travel —
for 1 minute.
