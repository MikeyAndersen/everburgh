Write a recap of the previous session to read aloud.

1. Read the newest note in `Sessions/` and any `Encounters/` notes from that date.
2. Write 150-250 words in **Danish**, past tense, told as a story rather than a list.
3. Name every PC who did something notable, and what they did — the players should hear
   their own character's name.
4. End on the open question or cliffhanger the session stopped on.
5. Do not reveal anything the players do not know. Check NPC notes for secrets before
   including a detail.

Output the recap only. No preamble, no headings.
