## Koncept

Genfødt af **[[Grumbar]]**, primordial gud for jord og udholdenhed. Kroppen er dækket af
rituelle sten-streger. Tavs, urokkelig, repræsenterer jordens tyngde. Hvor Everburghs
portaler og Ironholds magi bøjer virkeligheden, er Merith det urokkelige fundament.

## Mechanics

### Glyph of the Broken Witness — REMOVED

> [!warning] No longer active
> Removed during **de tre tableauer**. Kept here for historical reference only.
> Do not offer this power to Merith in play.

A cracked eye with a jagged "X" through the core, violet-grey like oil on glass. Flared
violently when lies were spoken nearby.

- *Rejection Pulse:* once per short rest, when targeted by a divination, enchantment or
  illusion spell, shatter it. Caster makes a DC 15 Wisdom save or is `Stunned` until the end
  of their next turn. Non-allies within 10 ft take 1d10 psychic damage. Could trigger even
  when unaware of being targeted.

### Curse — Echo Paradox

Temporal instability from breaking the prison's control. Sometimes loses his reaction, or a
temporal echo of him appears and speaks his next move aloud, betraying it.

**Curse removal:** must sleep on bare earth so Grumbar can slowly erode the magic away.

## Items

- **Band of Billaro** — held very dear. Lost once before; a copy returned to him in his
  reborn, time-hopped form.

## Timeline

Died with the whole party in their first encounter with [[Archmage Malcer Tharanis]]. Jumped
to a timeline where he had not been killed and did not recognise [[Ironhold Bastille]]. Has
since met the prison again, reunified his memory, and accepted that he is not the original
Merith — he has taken that man and his memories into himself.
